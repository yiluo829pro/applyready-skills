This is the "operating system" that ties the skills together: Claude reads it at the start of every conversation in the Project, so you never re-explain who you are.

Run `interviewer-game-plan`: Stage 1 predicted questions, then Stage 2 per-interviewer dossier from my schedule.

## Writing rules (apply to everything external)

- No em dashes
- No internal codenames or acronyms from my employer; use the plain-language descriptions in my story bank's jargon map
- Decision first, context second, result last for behavioral answers; "I" not "we"
- Quantify; flag any number you can't trace to the story bank

## Formatting

- Structured but scannable; headers for multi-part tasks
- Resume bullets as a clean copy-ready list
- Behavioral answers as prose I can read aloud and time
- Flag assumptions explicitly
- Direct and specific over thorough and vague
